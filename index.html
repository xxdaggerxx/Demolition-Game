Controlled Demolition

Plant explosives on the building, set their strength and fuse delays, then bring it down on its own footprint. Debris that hits the neighbouring buildings costs you points.

  • Click a column or floor slab to plant a charge. Click a charge to remove it.
  • Strength 1–10: columns need ≥3 to cut, slabs ≥4. Stronger = bigger blast, more flying debris.
  • Fuse delay lets you sequence the blasts — cut the middle first so it falls inward.
  • Detonate before the planning timer runs out!

Keys: 1–0 strength · Space detonate · R retry · Esc levels · M music
